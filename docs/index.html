About

Curious by nature.
Built to help you grow.

I started Inland NW AI because I genuinely love figuring out problems, and right now, AI is one of the most interesting tools available to solve them. My approach isn't to show up with a pre-packaged solution. It's to sit down, understand what you're dealing with, and explore what AI could actually do to help.

My background sits at the intersection of two things that don't always go together: the behavioral side of how people learn and adopt technology, and the applied technical skills to actually implement and build tools. That combination matters. Most organizations don't fail at AI because of the technology, they fail because the people side isn't handled well. I know both sides of that equation.

I work with solo operators, growing companies, nonprofits, and established organizations across Spokane, Coeur d'Alene, and the Inland Northwest. Whether you're a one-person shop that needs a website built, a team optimizing your operations, or a larger organization working to get real adoption across your people, I'm ready to help you explore and solve what's in front of you.
